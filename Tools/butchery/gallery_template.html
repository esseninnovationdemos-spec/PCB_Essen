How these were made

Cameras, not eyeballs

Every viewpoint is computed from plant_layout.py: each chamber is shot from its own corner at a stand-off derived from its footprint, and each line from behind the head of its group looking down its axis. That is not tidiness — three earlier hand-placed interior shots landed inside a wall or inside a machine, and a computed camera cannot end up buried when the layout moves.

The plant views use a roofless build of the same level. A roofed building cannot be photographed from above, and the plan is most legible from directly overhead; walls, columns and rafters stay, so it still reads as a building rather than a floor plan.

Stills render at eight spatial samples and one temporal. A held camera gets its quality from spatial samples — temporal ones buy motion blur a still does not want and cost exactly the same — with 24 warm-up frames so Lumen converges before the frame that is kept.

Shown here at 720 px so the page stays inside an artifact's size ceiling. The full-resolution set is on disk in Tools/butchery/showcase/.
